FAQ

Affiliate Questions

How much can I earn as an affiliate?
You earn between 8% and 15% commission on every sale depending on your tier. There is no cap on earnings — top affiliates earn thousands monthly.
When do I get paid?
Affiliate commissions are paid out monthly. Once your balance reaches the minimum threshold ($50), you can request a payout.
Do I need to be a trader to join?
No! Anyone can join our affiliate program. Whether you're an influencer, content creator, or trader — you can earn by referring others.
How do I track my referrals?
You get access to a real-time affiliate dashboard where you can track clicks, signups, purchases, and earnings.
Is there a cookie duration?
Yes. Our tracking cookie lasts 60 days. If someone clicks your link and purchases within 60 days, you earn the commission.
